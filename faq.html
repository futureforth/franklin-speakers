Booking a speaker in Franklin, TN.

Answers for event planners hiring keynote speakers in Franklin, Nashville and Middle Tennessee.

01

How do I book a speaker for an event in Franklin, Tennessee?

Choose the speaker who fits your audience and contact them through their profile on FranklinSpeakers.com. Share your event date and goals, and mention that you found them on Franklin Speakers. Ask about availability and special event pricing for your program.

02

How much does it cost to hire a speaker?

Fees vary by speaker, session format, and event scope. Contact your chosen speaker for a quote and mention FranklinSpeakers.com to ask about special event pricing. Confirm what the quote includes, such as preparation, travel, and any additional sessions.

03

Which speaker is the right fit for my audience?

Start with the challenge your audience faces and what you want them to take away. Compare the speakers’ areas of expertise, review their profiles, and discuss your goals with your preferred speaker before booking.

04

What topics do Franklin Speakers cover?

05

Are the speakers local to Franklin or the Nashville area?

Yes. Every speaker on Franklin Speakers is based in the Franklin, Tennessee area and serves events across Nashville and Middle Tennessee. Booking a local speaker can reduce or eliminate travel costs. Ask your chosen speaker about availability on your event date.

06

Can I watch a speaker before I book?

Ask your preferred speaker for a TEDx talk, keynote clip, or other speaking video. Watching a sample can help you assess their delivery, message, and fit for your audience. Your chosen speaker can share the most relevant examples for your event.
